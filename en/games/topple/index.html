Topple

Archery game for players who like physics

You draw the bow and topple the tower in the fewest shots. Every block has its own mass, friction and bounce.

What we built

  • 13 hand-built levels with saved progress
  • Three materials: wood, stone and glass
  • Glass that shatters and lets arrows through
  • Eight sounds generated from maths, about 800 KB
  • A trajectory preview on the same gravity
The hard part

The trajectory preview and the real physics must agree exactly. One gravity model draws both. The dotted arc is a promise the shot keeps.
